DOCUMENT CONTROL: BANKING COMPLIANCE & TECHNICAL STANDARDS (RBI-SPEC-2026-v1)
DOMAIN: Commercial Banking - Enterprise Loan Approval & Risk Engine (India)

1. STATUTORY COMPLIANCE & REGULATORY MANDATES (RESERVE BANK OF INDIA)
1.1 RBI Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) & AML Norms
- Pursuant to RBI KYC Master Directions, all commercial loan applications with requested credit limits exceeding INR 10,00,000 must undergo mandatory, automated identity verification (Central KYC / CKYC).
- Applicant entities, directors, and Ultimate Beneficial Owners (UBOs) must be dynamically screened against RBI Defaulter Lists, SEBI Debarred Lists, and MHA Sanction Watchlists prior to credit scoring.

1.2 RBI Digital Lending Guidelines & Fair Practices Code
- Automated credit scoring algorithms must maintain complete explainability and transparency.
- If an application is rejected or subjected to elevated interest rates, the system must automatically generate explicit Key Fact Statements (KFS) and adverse action notices detailing the mathematical risk metrics used.

1.3 Information Technology Act (2000) & RBI Cyber Security Framework for Banks
- All applicant financial records, GST returns, ITR filings, and Aadhaar/PAN identifiers must be encrypted using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII) must be masked across non-production environments and stored in compliance with Indian Data Localisation mandates.

1.4 Governance, Auditability & Lineage (RBI Audit Mandates)
- The system must implement an immutable, write-once-read-many (WORM) audit log detailing every risk score calculation, rule evaluation, system decision, and manual credit officer override.
- Audit logs must be retained for a mandatory statutory period of ten (10) years and be made available for RBI regulatory compliance inspections.

2. CORE SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & INTEGRATION CONSTRAINTS
2.1 Legacy Core-Banking & Fintech Interoperability
- The system must maintain real-time REST/SOAP API integration with legacy Core Banking Systems (CBS) and NPCI/Account Aggregator networks to pull applicant account balances, GSTIN data, and transaction histories.
- Legacy system timeouts must handle retries gracefully without duplicating transaction records or risk scores.

2.2 Multi-Tier Approval Workflows
- Automated approval is restricted to low-risk applications under INR 50,00,000.
- Applications between INR 50,00,000 and INR 5,00,00,000 require dual-control sign-off from a Senior Credit Officer.
- High-risk applications exceeding INR 5,00,00,000 or failing automated KYC screening must be escalated to the Executive Risk Management Committee.

2.3 Calculation Integrity & Operational Reliability
- High availability is mandated (99.99% uptime) with zero tolerance for calculation discrepancies in interest compounding, Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR), or Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA).

2.4 Bank Capital & Exposure Concentration Limits (RBI Large Exposures Framework)
- Before sanctioning any corporate loan, the system must aggregate the bank's existing credit commitments to the applicant and its connected group entities against real-time Tier-1 capital availability.
- Applications that breach RBI single-borrower (20% of Tier-1 capital) or group-borrower (25% of Tier-1 capital) concentration caps must be automatically blocked to prevent systemic risk accumulation.
